# Assignment Index

## Weekly Labs

Students complete regular weekly labs to practice the current programming concept.

## Quizzes or Checkpoints

Short checkpoints may be used to check understanding of concepts, code tracing, debugging, and vocabulary.

## Reflection Assignments

Reflection assignments ask students to explain what they tried, what worked, what failed, and what they learned.

## Projects

The course will build toward a small final Python project.

## Final Submission

The final submission should include working code, evidence of testing, and a short explanation or presentation.
