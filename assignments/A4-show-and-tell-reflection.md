# Reusable assignment: show-and-tell reflection

**Template based on recurring Show and Tell activities and the spring 2026 journal.**

Demonstrate one working program or meaningful attempt, explain what you learned, and respond to: what else did you learn, what could have gone better, and what made the experience special. A written response, discussion post, or short presentation may be used depending on delivery mode.

Historically this is low-stakes/pass-fail or attendance-linked, with 2026 using a repeated four-question journal worth 10 points.
