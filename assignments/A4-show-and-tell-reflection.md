# Show and Tell reflection

## Purpose

Demonstrate one working program (or a meaningful honest attempt) and reflect
on it — the Friday active-work slot in this course's weekly rhythm
(`docs/course-ethos.md`).

## Do

1. Demonstrate one working program or meaningful attempt from this week —
   in discussion, a short presentation, or a written/discussion post,
   depending on how your section runs Show and Tell.
2. Fill out the four-question journal below in your own words.
3. If your section pairs this with peer feedback, see the Friday feedback
   report note below — it's graded separately.

## The journal (10 points, current format)

Each question is graded individually, pass/fail:

| # | Question | Points |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What did you learn? | 3 |
| 2 | What else did you learn? | 3 |
| 3 | What could have gone better? | 2 |
| 4 | What made something feel special? | 2 |

Answer each in your own words, specific to this week — "it went fine" does
not pass; naming an actual moment, bug, or realization does. Any honest,
specific answer passes; you don't need a success story for questions 3–4.

## Separately: the Friday feedback report

If your section's Friday session includes peer feedback, the *quality of
feedback you gave* is graded as its own 5% category
(`docs/grading-model.md`), not as part of this journal. Keep the two
separate: this journal is about your own work; the feedback report is about
how well you responded to someone else's.

## Support and integrity

This is low-stakes by design — the point is honest reflection, not a
polished essay. If you're not sure what counts as "something to demonstrate"
in a slow week, ask; a real attempt that didn't fully work is still valid
material for questions 1–3.
