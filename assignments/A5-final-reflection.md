# Final reflection

## Purpose

Look back honestly on the semester — this is the final, in place of a
traditional exam (`docs/course-ethos.md`, decided 2026-07-15). Worth 10% of
the course grade (`docs/grading-model.md`).

## Do

1. Pick **five** prompts from the bank below (or propose an
   instructor-approved equivalent) — at least one from each of the four
   categories.
2. Answer each in your own words. These are pass/fail: any honest, specific
   answer earns full credit; vague or generic answers ("it was a good
   semester") don't, but there's no single "correct" answer to chase.
3. Share one favorite meme, quote, or moment from the semester — optional,
   but a nice way to close it out.
4. If your section's Coding Odyssey Week 17 work includes the **Final
   Debrief** and Judgment Log review
   (`assignments/odyssey_gates/week-17.md`), that *is* this reflection's
   Odyssey-specific half — don't write two separate reflections. Use one of
   your five prompts below for the rest of the semester's non-Odyssey
   material (classmates, career steps, general growth) so the two don't
   overlap.

## Prompt bank — pick 5, at least one per category

**Growth in programming**
- Describe the area where you grew the most as a programmer this semester,
  and what specifically helped you improve.
- What's something you can do now with code that you couldn't do in Week 1?
- What was the hardest concept to click, and what finally made it click?

**A project or obstacle**
- Describe the biggest obstacle you overcame this semester and what kept
  you working at it.
- Explain your favorite assignment and why it stuck with you.
- Walk through one bug that taught you something real about how the
  computer (or you) actually think.

**Learning habits and community**
- How were you able to help a classmate this semester?
- Describe your best tools or habits for getting support when you were
  stuck.
- What would you tell a friend taking this class next semester, that you
  wish someone had told you?

**Next steps**
- What steps did you take this semester toward your career, and why did
  they matter to you?
- Describe your plan for learning about job opportunities or companies
  you're interested in.
- Name one specific thing (a certification, a project, a habit) you want to
  build on next semester, and why.

## Support and integrity

This is pass/fail and open-book/open-note/open-everything — take the time
you need, and it's fine to revise and resubmit if you change your mind
later. If a prompt above doesn't fit your semester, swap it for one that
does; the categories matter more than the exact wording.
