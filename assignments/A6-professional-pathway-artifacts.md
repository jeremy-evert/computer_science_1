# Reusable assignment set: professional pathway artifacts

**Template based on the recurring 2023–26 career strand.**

Submit the artifacts that fit the current course plan: unofficial transcript; advisor/progress or degree check; semester-by-semester degree plan; resume; dream-job/role research; professional skill-gap analysis; GitHub and LinkedIn profile; and selected LinkedIn Learning or networking evidence.

The archive frames these as real planning and evidence-building, not generic career worksheets. Earlier semesters use the Dream Job Project and two project parts; later semesters break the pathway into smaller artifacts and tie it to the student’s target role.

## Fall 2026 staging (Decision, 2026-07-22)

This set is staged across the semester, not submitted all at once in Week
16. See `reports/005_apply_pre_semester_decisions.md`.

1. **Week 1** — establish baseline artifacts: unofficial transcript/degree
   audit, advisor/progress check, a preliminary degree plan, a current
   (even incomplete) resume, current GitHub/LinkedIn, and an initial
   dream-job/role interest. Largely covered by `semester_kickoff_week`'s
   A01–A09 set.
2. **Week 14** — brief update pass: refresh dream-job/role research, a
   skill-gap comparison against it, resume update, GitHub/LinkedIn update,
   degree-plan update if anything changed.
3. **Week 15** — completion and submission. Independent work, asynchronous
   week (instructor traveling); revise, organize, and complete work already
   begun rather than creating artifacts from scratch. Full submission
   includes all of the above plus a brief reflection on next steps.

Week 16 does not carry this assignment — it's due in Week 15.
