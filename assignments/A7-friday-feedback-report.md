# Friday feedback report

## Purpose

Practice giving useful, specific feedback — the other half of the Friday
Show and Tell slot in this course's weekly rhythm (`docs/course-ethos.md`).
`assignments/A4-show-and-tell-reflection.md` grades what you demonstrated
and how you reflected on your own work; this report grades the quality of
the feedback you *gave* someone else, as its own 5% category
(`docs/grading-model.md`). Authored 2026-08-06 (course_foundry prompt 026)
-- previously referenced by `docs/course-ethos.md`, `docs/grading-model.md`,
`templates/T5-start-here.md`, and both A3 and A4's "Separately:" sections,
but never itself written.

## Do

1. During Friday Show and Tell, watch or read at least one classmate's
   demonstration (in-class discussion, presentation, or written/discussion
   post, depending on how your section runs it).
2. Give them feedback in the moment (discussion) or in writing (post/
   comment), specific to what they actually showed.
3. Record, in your own words, what feedback you gave and why you gave it.

## What you submit: the feedback report

This is graded on the *quality of the feedback you gave*, not on your own
project (`docs/grading-model.md`, 5% of the course grade). Include:

- Who/what you gave feedback on, briefly (no need to name classmates if
  your section prefers anonymity -- "the Investigation Bureau project I
  watched" is enough).
- The feedback itself, in your own words -- not "looks good," a specific
  observation ("the loop recalculates the same total three times; a
  running variable would avoid that" is feedback; "nice job" is not).
- Why you gave that feedback -- what you noticed, and why it seemed worth
  saying.
- One thing you'd want the same kind of feedback on on your own project,
  next time.

## Separately: the Show and Tell reflection

Your own project's demonstration and reflection are graded separately,
under `assignments/A4-show-and-tell-reflection.md`. Don't fold that
journal into this report -- this report is only about the feedback you
gave someone else.

## Support and integrity

If your section's Friday format doesn't include peer feedback some weeks
(e.g. low attendance, a scheduling change), this report may be dropped for
that week -- check with your instructor rather than inventing feedback you
didn't actually give.
