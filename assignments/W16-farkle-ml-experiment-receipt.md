## Do

Fill out every section below in your own words. Two to four sentences
per numbered item is normal — this is not a report.

### 1. Your human strategy

State the rule you used before seeing the machine, in one plain
sentence (e.g., "bank once I hit 400, unless I'm close to winning, then
I bank sooner").

### 2. What you ran

- Command(s) you ran, with the `--seed` and `--games`/`--turns` values
  you used.
- Number of simulated games/turns behind your evidence.

### 3. Evidence: strategy vs. strategy, or before vs. after learning

Paste (or summarize in a small table) the actual printed comparison —
win rate, average score, and Farkle rate for both sides.

### 4. Interpretation questions (answer at least 3 of the 5 from the lesson)

Use `lessons/10-farkle-ml.md` §6. Quote or paraphrase the specific
question you're answering.

### 5. One surprise

Something in the numbers you did not expect.

### 6. One limitation

Something the machine's table does not account for (hint: what's in the
state dictionary, and what isn't).
