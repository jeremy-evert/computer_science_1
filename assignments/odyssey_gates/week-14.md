## Full Trail Debrief (mandatory this week — new, not in the original A2 template)

Weeks 12-13 were the deliberate refactor crisis: every major noun in your
world became a class. This Debrief is where you make that refactor's real
cost and payoff explicit, not just move past it. Answer specifically, not
in general terms:

1. **What broke** when you moved from dict/list entries to classes? Name a
   real bug or awkward moment, not "nothing, it went smoothly" (if it
   really did go smoothly, say what made it easy — that's useful evidence
   too, but be specific about why).
2. **Why** did it break — was it a design choice made back in Week 10-11
   that didn't anticipate objects, a Python mechanic you hadn't used before
   (`self`, mutation, references), or something else?
3. **What would you design differently**, starting from Week 1 with what
   you know now? This is the actual point of the Debrief — not
   self-criticism, evidence of a changed mental model.

This pairs with the already-scheduled Friday show-and-tell reflection tie-in
(`planning/week-14.md`, `assignments/A4-show-and-tell-reflection.md`) —
don't write two separate reflections; this Debrief *is* that week's
reflection, deepened.
