# Odyssey Gate — Week 17: Finals — the world, looked back on

**Concept:** none new — final portfolio and Coding Odyssey show-and-tell,
course wrap-up (`planning/week-17-finals.md`). **Instruments:** final
Debrief + **Judgment Log checkpoint 3 (final)** — see
`docs/curriculum/judgment_toolkit.md` §4/§5.

## Final Debrief

Same cyclical shape as every prior Debrief (assess, evaluate, plan,
monitor, reflect — `judgment_toolkit.md` §4), applied to the whole
semester:

1. What's the real difference between your Week 1 founding charter and
   your world as it exists now? Not "it has more features" — what actually
   changed about how you think about building it?
2. Pick one Decide/Compare moment (Week 11 or Week 16) and say whether you'd
   still make the same call today. Why or why not?
3. What's still on your "known debt" list, and why didn't it get fixed?
   (An honest, unresolved debt list is a pass condition, not a penalty —
   see grading notes below.)

## Judgment Log checkpoint 3 (final)

Review the whole World Bible as a record, not just this week's entry:
founding charter present, a real line for every week's gate (not
retroactively filled in the last week), a running known-debt list that
actually changed over the semester rather than staying static. This is
also the student's own AI-practice "Routine" evidence — worth pointing out
to students directly: the same discipline asked of their fictional world is
the discipline this actual course roadmap (`ROADMAP.md`) is built with.

Pairs with the already-scheduled full show-and-tell and
`assignments/A5-final-reflection.md` (`planning/week-17-finals.md`) — this
is that reflection's Odyssey-specific half, not a separate assignment.

## Grading notes (for whoever grades this)

- A student who honestly reports unresolved known debt and explains why
  should score as well as one who fixed everything — the point is an
  honest record of judgment over the semester, not a spotless project. This
  matches this course's own existing grading tone
  (`docs/philosophy/teaching-patterns.md`) and Jeremy's standing framing
  from the 2026-07-22/23 Marker calibration work: the value is the
  learning, not point-perfect execution.
