# Reusable assignment: paired programming and explanation

**Template based on fall 2024 and fall/spring 2025 repeated paired assignments.**

Work with a partner on the current chapter problems. Take turns driving and explaining; record the problem, approach, code changes, tests, and each partner’s contribution. Present progress to the partner/class and identify any assistance or AI/resource use.

This format was added to nearly every chapter in the 2024–25 offerings and is not visible in the earlier archive. It may be omitted when schedule or enrollment makes pairing impractical.
