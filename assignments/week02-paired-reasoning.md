# Week 2 paired reasoning evidence

## Purpose

Practice separating an AI proposal from independently checked evidence.

## Instructions

Choose one small claim from the Local AI or Python work. With a partner or an
asynchronous peer exchange, record the proposal, the independent check, what
the evidence supports, one limitation, and the next question. Each person
submits their own 150–250 word receipt and names the partner exchange without
including private correspondence.

## Source and path

Use the numbered Week 2 lessons and `planning/week-02.md`; submit this receipt
with the Wednesday evidence path. Online students use the same fields through
`planning/week-02-online.md`.
