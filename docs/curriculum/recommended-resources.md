## Why CS50P

CS50P scored highest of every evaluated no-cost resource against the
pinned C01–C13 core (seven capability IDs at the top score, the only
candidate with a dedicated Unit Tests week and a dedicated OOP week, and
the strongest built-in autograded practice loop). Full scoring detail:
`reports/011_cs1_resource_coverage_matrix.csv` /
`reports/011_cs1_resource_coverage_matrix.json`. License: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0,
confirmed at `cs50.harvard.edu/python/license/` — compatible with linking
and noncommercial, share-alike adaptation for this public, noncommercial
university course. No CS50P body content is reproduced here; every entry
above is a link only.

## What fills CS50P's holes

- **Git/GitHub (C12):** CS50P has no Git/GitHub content at all — GitHub
  Skills' "Introduction to GitHub" (MIT license) fills this, matching
  Week 14's existing focus.
- **AI-aware programming (C13):** CS50P's coverage is thin — GitHub Docs'
  "Review AI-generated code" (CC BY 4.0) is a partial fill; the course's
  own Monday Moments AI Fluency lenses and the Week 14 Full Trail Debrief
  disclosure requirement carry most of the actual assessed outcome.
- **Files/persistence (C09):** CS50P's File I/O week covers `open`,
  `list`, `sorted`, `with`, and CSV; *Automate the Boring Stuff*'s
  file/CSV/JSON chapters are a light supplemental read for anything CS50P
  doesn't fully close.
- **Technical judgment (C11):** no external resource beats the course's own
  Decide/Compare instrument and Judgment Log
  (`docs/curriculum/judgment_toolkit.md`) — CS50P is reinforcement only,
  never the primary evidence source for this capability.

- Do not require CS50P enrollment, its full problem-set sequence, or its
  certificate.
- Do not copy/rehost CS50P (or any other licensed/CC-restricted) body
  content into this repo or into Canvas/Savnac — link only.
