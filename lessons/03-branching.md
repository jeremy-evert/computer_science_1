# Branching and decision-making

## Objectives

- Translate conditions into Boolean expressions.
- Use `if`/`elif`/`else` to handle alternatives.
- Test boundary and invalid cases.

## Key content

Comparisons, Boolean operators, nested decisions, and traceable decision tables.

## Historical materials

Chapter 4 is stable from fall 2022 through spring 2026. Historical labs include smallest-number and interstate-highway classification problems; branching is also a common early project ingredient.
