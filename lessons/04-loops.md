# Loops and repetition

## Objectives

- Select `for` or `while` for a repetition problem.
- Use ranges, accumulators, sentinels, and loop conditions.
- Diagnose off-by-one and non-terminating loops.

## Key content

Counter-controlled and sentinel-controlled loops, repeated input, accumulation, and simple validation.

## Historical materials

Chapter 5 recurs in the modern sequence. Repeated labs include output ranges, Mad Libs, and iterative cost or game behavior.
