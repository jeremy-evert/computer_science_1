# Classes, objects, and modules

## Objectives

- Read and write a simple class with attributes and methods.
- Explain object state and method calls.
- Separate related definitions into modules.

## Key content

Constructors, `self`, instance state, methods, imports, and a small object-oriented model.

## Historical materials

Chapter 9 is repeatedly split into Round 1 and Round 2 from fall 2023 through spring 2026. Fall 2021 includes artwork-label and vending-machine style class work; the syllabus places classes after source-code management.
