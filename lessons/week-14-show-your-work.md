# Show Your Work (Week 14, optional, no new deliverable)

By the end of this course you will have made something worth showing. This week connects to the Git-backed project receipt you are already building: your history, commits, pushes and GitHub page are exactly what a stranger would see.

Read the one-page guide first: https://jeremy-evert.github.io/swosu-computing/show-your-work/

What to look for this week:

- **Explain it:** the first lines of your README say what the project is, who it is for, and how to run it.
- **Say how you made it:** a short note naming the tools you used, AI included, what you asked for, and what you checked yourself. This is the same honesty as your AI-aware verification.
- **Credit it:** list libraries, images, sounds and starter code you did not write.
- **Look at the history:** a secret or a private path you deleted last week is still in the commits.

Before you publish anything, tick the checklist: https://github.com/jeremy-evert/oeqa_spell_roller_public/blob/main/CHECKLIST.md

Pick one thing you made, find the first checklist box it fails, and fix that one box. That is the whole task.
