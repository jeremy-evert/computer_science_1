# COMSC 1033 Fall 2026 — 10–15 Minute Block Map

**The master target list for the content pipeline.** Every in-person 10–15
minute block for the semester, dated against the official academic calendar
(`archive/2026-27-academic-calendar-unofficial.pdf`) and section schedule
(COMSC-1033-1415, MWF 10:00–10:50, Stafford Center 125, Aug 17 – Dec 11).

Holiday facts baked in: **Labor Day Mon Sep 7** (no Monday, week 4) ·
**Fall Break begins Wed Oct 14, 10 PM** (no Friday, week 9) · **Thanksgiving
begins Tue Nov 24, 10 PM** (Monday only, week 15) · **Finals Dec 7–11**.

**Block counts: 15 Monday Moments · 15 Wacky Wednesdays · 14 Fun Fridays =
44 strand blocks**, plus 15 Monday lecture packages.

Per block, the pipeline produces (priority order): **1** digest doc
(LaTeX/Markdown) → **2** Beamer deck (≤15 min, presented via pdfpc/pympress
timer) → **3** podcast → **4** graphic novel chapter → **5** *(pipe dream)*
3–5 min AI-animated video. Checkboxes track the first three; 4–5 ride along
when the pipeline can.
