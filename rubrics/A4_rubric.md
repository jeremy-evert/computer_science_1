# Rubric: Show and Tell Reflection

- Paired assignment: `assignments/A4-show-and-tell-reflection.md`
- Total points: 10

Each question is graded individually, pass/fail, per the assignment's own
"The journal" table — full credit for an honest, specific answer; zero
credit for a non-answer ("it went fine") or a blank response. No partial
credit within a question; the point values below are the assignment's own
declared per-question weights (3/3/2/2 = 10).

## Criteria

### 1. What did you learn? (3 pts)

- **3 — Full credit:** Names an actual moment, bug, or realization from
  this week's work, specific enough that a reader could tell what
  happened.
- **0 — No credit:** Blank, or a non-answer ("it went fine") with nothing
  specific to this week.

### 2. What else did you learn? (3 pts)

- **3 — Full credit:** A second, distinct specific moment or realization
  (not a restatement of question 1).
- **0 — No credit:** Blank, a restatement of question 1, or a non-answer.

### 3. What could have gone better? (2 pts)

- **2 — Full credit:** Names a specific thing that could have gone
  better this week — a real attempt that didn't fully work is valid
  material here.
- **0 — No credit:** Blank, or a non-answer with nothing specific
  named.

### 4. What made something feel special? (2 pts)

- **2 — Full credit:** Names a specific moment, however small, that felt
  special this week — no success story is required.
- **0 — No credit:** Blank, or a non-answer with nothing specific
  named.
