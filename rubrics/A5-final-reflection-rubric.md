# Rubric: Final Reflection

- Paired assignment: `assignments/A5-final-reflection.md`
- Total points: 10

## Criteria

### 1. Five reflection prompts, answered in your own voice (10 pts)

- **10 — Full credit:** Answered five prompts from the bank (or one
  instructor-approved substitute of the student's own), at least one from
  each of the four categories, in the student's own words. Generous bar by
  design, matching the assignment's own stated policy — any honest,
  specific answer earns full credit; there is no single "correct" answer
  to chase.
- **5 — Partial credit:** Fewer than five prompts answered, or the
  one-per-category spread wasn't met, but what was submitted is honest and
  in the student's own voice.
- **0 — No credit:** Left blank, or every answer is generic/templated with
  no identifiable connection to the student's own semester (e.g. "it was a
  good semester").

### 2. Genuinely specific and reflective (2 pts) — extra credit

- **2 — Full credit:** At least one answer names a specific, identifiable
  detail — a moment, a bug, a person, a decision — rather than staying
  general; the kind of specificity that could only have come from this
  student's actual semester.
- **1 — Partial credit:** Some specificity present, but most answers stay
  general.
- **0 — No credit:** Every answer stays generic even where specificity was
  clearly possible.

This criterion is a bonus layer on top of criterion 1's full credit, not a
stricter bar for it — a student can earn all 10 base points with generous,
honest-but-general answers, and only reaches for these 2 extra points if
they choose to go further.
