# Rubric: Professional Pathway — Week 14 Update

- Paired assignment: `assignments/A6-professional-pathway-artifacts.md`
  (Week 14 update pass)
- Total points: 10

Graded on the reasoning behind the update, not artifact polish — the
practice of revisiting and justifying a career plan is the point, per
Jeremy's own framing ("this is where we can help them establish practices
that they can use as they continue to grow in their profession").

## Criteria

### 1. Refreshed research and skill-gap reasoning (4 pts)

- **4 — Full credit:** Target-role research is genuinely refreshed (not
  re-pasted from Week 1), and the skill-gap comparison explains *why* each
  named gap matters to that specific role, not just that a gap exists.
- **2 — Partial credit:** Research or skill-gap comparison is present but
  stays generic, or doesn't clearly connect back to the target role.
- **0 — No credit:** Missing, or unchanged from the Week 1 baseline.

### 2. Updated artifacts — resume, GitHub, LinkedIn, degree plan (3 pts)

- **3 — Full credit:** At least two of the four artifacts (resume, GitHub,
  LinkedIn, degree plan) show a real, dated update made since Week 1.
- **1.5 — Partial credit:** One artifact updated, or updates are present
  but not clearly dated/attributable to this pass.
- **0 — No credit:** No artifact shows any change since Week 1.

### 3. Changelog: what changed since Week 1, and why (3 pts)

- **3 — Full credit:** Names the specific changes made since the Week 1
  baseline and explains the reasoning behind each one — why the target
  role shifted, why a resume line was reworded, why a project was added —
  not just a list of edits.
- **1.5 — Partial credit:** Lists changes but doesn't explain the
  reasoning behind them.
- **0 — No credit:** No changelog/diff entry submitted.
