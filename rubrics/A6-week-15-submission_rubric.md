# Rubric: Professional Pathway — Week 15 Submission

- Paired assignment: `assignments/A6-professional-pathway-artifacts.md`
  (Week 15 completion and submission)
- Total points: 10

Completion + submission of the full artifact set begun Week 1 and updated
Week 14, plus a reflection on next steps — not new-from-scratch artifacts
(`assignments/A6-professional-pathway-artifacts.md`).

## Criteria

### 1. Full artifact set submitted and organized (5 pts)

- **5 — Full credit:** The complete set is submitted together and legible
  as one package — transcript/degree audit, degree plan, resume,
  GitHub/LinkedIn, target-role research, skill-gap analysis.
- **2.5 — Partial credit:** Most of the set is submitted, but one or two
  pieces are missing or hard to locate.
- **0 — No credit:** Set is largely incomplete or not submitted.

### 2. Next-steps reflection: reasoning, not just a list (5 pts)

- **5 — Full credit:** Names specific next steps (a certification, an
  application timeline, a skill to build) and explains why each one
  matters to this student's own plan, not a generic list of "things
  people should do."
- **2.5 — Partial credit:** Names next steps but doesn't explain the
  reasoning behind them.
- **0 — No credit:** No next-steps reflection submitted.
