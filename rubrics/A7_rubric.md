# Rubric: Friday Feedback Report

- Paired assignment: `assignments/A7-friday-feedback-report.md`
- Total points: 10

## Criteria

### 1. Specificity (4 pts) — the feedback itself

- **4 — Full credit:** Feedback names a concrete detail of the classmate's
  actual work (a specific choice, bug, or design decision) rather than a
  generic reaction. A reader who never saw the demo could tell roughly
  what was shown from the feedback alone.
- **2 — Partial credit:** Feedback is topically relevant but stays vague
  ("the logic was a bit confusing") without naming the specific thing that
  was confusing.
- **0 — No credit:** Feedback is generic praise/criticism with no
  identifiable connection to what was actually demonstrated ("looks
  good," "needs work").

### 2. Reasoning (3 pts) — why the feedback was given

- **3 — Full credit:** Explains *why* the observation mattered — what
  problem it would cause, or what it did well and why that's worth
  repeating.
- **1.5 — Partial credit:** States the observation but not the reasoning
  behind it.
- **0 — No credit:** No reasoning given, or the reasoning doesn't connect
  to the stated observation.

### 3. Reflection back (3 pts) — applying it to your own work

- **3 — Full credit:** Names one specific thing the student would want
  the same kind of feedback on, tied to their own project (not a generic
  "I should get feedback more").
- **1.5 — Partial credit:** Names something but it's generic or not
  clearly tied to their own project.
- **0 — No credit:** Left blank, or unrelated to the feedback given.
