| Criterion | Pass condition |
|---|---|
| Relevant list | List holds multiple entities meaningfully tied to the world, not scratch data. |
| Built incrementally | ≥2 real `.append()`-style additions across the run, not one static literal. |

**All three present → pass.** Fully mechanical.
