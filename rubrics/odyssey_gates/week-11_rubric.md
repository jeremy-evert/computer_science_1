# Odyssey Gate — Week 11 rubric: Quick Check + Decide/Compare #1

Matches `docs/curriculum/judgment_toolkit.md` §1/§3. Two separately-graded
parts.

## Part 1 — Quick Check (pass/fail, mechanical-checkable)

| Criterion | Pass condition |
|---|---|
| Dict-based lookup | A dict is used to retrieve an entity by key, replacing manual search. |
| Real data | Dict is populated from real world data, not a hardcoded literal. |
| Lookup demonstrated | At least one successful key lookup is shown returning a real result. |

**All three present → pass.** Fully mechanical.

## Part 2 — Decide/Compare #1 (separately graded, not pass/fail)

| Band | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| Strong | Names a specific, real trade-off tied to this student's actual data shape (not a generic "dicts are O(1)" line); choice visibly follows the comparison. |
| Solid | Real trade-off named, but generic or thinly connected to the student's own world. |
| Not yet | Comparison is cosmetic — reasons given after the fact that don't actually distinguish the alternatives, or no real alternative considered. |

## Grading notes

- Do not require the "textbook correct" choice (dict) to be the only
  passing answer for Decide/Compare — a student who genuinely argues for a
  list in a specific case (e.g., small fixed collection, insertion-order
  matters more than lookup speed) and defends it well should score Strong.
  The instrument grades the *quality of the comparison*, not agreement with
  the expected answer.
- The World Bible one-line log entry (this week's assignment) is a
  required but not-separately-scored part of the submission — tracked
  across the semester and reviewed as a whole at Week 17's checkpoint
  (`rubrics/odyssey_gates/week-17_rubric.md`), not graded week-by-week
  here.
- This week's assignment also has an open "light Build" continuation
  (same holistic, ungraded shape as other weeks' Part 2) alongside the
  scored Decide/Compare #1 above — not part of either scored part here.
