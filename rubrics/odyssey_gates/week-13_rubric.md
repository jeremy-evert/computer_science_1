# Odyssey Gate — Week 13 rubric: Quick Check (pass/fail) + light Build

Matches `docs/curriculum/judgment_toolkit.md` §1/§2.

## Part 1 — Quick Check (pass/fail)

| Criterion | Pass condition |
|---|---|
| Two roles | ≥2 distinct classes, or 2 meaningfully different instance roles if the genre only naturally supports one class. |
| Cross-object call | A method call crosses between two objects (not two isolated instances). |
| Real effect | The interaction changes real state or produces a real computed result. |

**All three present → pass.**

**Grading route:** "two roles" and "cross-object call" are mechanical-
checkable; **"two meaningfully different instance roles" needs a human
read** when a submission only has one class — don't auto-fail a genre
that's genuinely thinner on natural noun-count (e.g., a very small Small
Business world) without checking whether the second role is a real design
choice or a missed gate.
