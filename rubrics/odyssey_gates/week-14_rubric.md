# Reasoning Odyssey Gate — Week 14 rubric: Synthesis Checkpoint 3 (full Build) + Full Trail Debrief (mandatory)

Matches `docs/curriculum/judgment_toolkit.md` §2/§4. Two separately-graded
parts, neither pass/fail — this week uses full rubrics, not a Quick Check.

## Part 1 — Full Build

Use the four-axis rubric from `judgment_toolkit.md` §2 (Functions / Concept
use / Explanation / Demonstrability) directly — no Week-14-specific
variant. "Concept use" here means real class interaction (Week 13's gate),
not just any code running.

## Part 2 — Full Trail Debrief (mandatory)

| Band | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| Strong | Names a specific, real break (or a specific, real reason nothing broke) with a concrete cause tied to an actual Week 10-13 design decision; the "what I'd design differently" answer shows a genuinely different mental model, not a restatement of what was already built. |
| Solid | Real answer to all three Debrief questions, but the cause analysis or the redesign answer stays generic ("I'd plan better" without saying what that means concretely). |
| Not yet | Answers are present but don't engage with the actual refactor — could have been written without having done Weeks 12-13 at all. |

## Grading notes

- A student who reports "nothing broke" should not automatically score low
  — but the rubric requires them to explain *why* it went smoothly (a good
  Week 10-11 design choice, prior OOP exposure, etc.), not just assert it.
- This is the one week where a thin World Bible log ("passed the gate, all
  good") is not sufficient — the Full Trail Debrief needs real prose, not
  a checklist line.
