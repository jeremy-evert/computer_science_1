What this week asks

This week's Reasoning Odyssey Gate asks your world to state its own founding condition — a settlement's opening food stock, a case's hours-cold clock, a starship's fuel remaining after a jump, a business's opening cash on hand. Every one of those is the same shape: a variable, an expression that computes something from it, and a sentence that prints the result.
