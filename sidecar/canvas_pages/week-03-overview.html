What this week asks

This week's Reasoning Odyssey Gate asks your world to make its first real decision: print a different line depending on some piece of world state — a settlement's food stock, a case's hours-cold clock, a starship's fuel remaining, a business's cash on hand. That's exactly what branching is: letting the value of an expression choose which lines of code run.
