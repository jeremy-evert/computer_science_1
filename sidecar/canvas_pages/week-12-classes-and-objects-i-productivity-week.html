Reasoning Odyssey Gate — Week 12 — The World Becomes Real Things (Round 1)

What are we learning? Classes and objects — turning a major noun you've been tracking as dict/list entries into a real class with a constructor and real behavior. This is the start of a deliberate refactor; full reckoning with multiple interacting classes is Week 13.

What am I building/changing? Turn at least one major noun already tracked in your Week 10–11 collection into a class, with a constructor (__init__) that sets real instance attributes, and at least one method beyond __init__.

How does my world connect? The dict/list entity from Weeks 10–11 becomes an actual object — the same data, now with behavior attached to it directly.

What evidence do I keep? One World Bible line: what gate you passed, what broke — refactors into classes tend to surface real bugs from the dict/list version; that's expected, log it.

Quick Check (pass/fail — all three required, no partial credit)

A class is defined with __init__ setting ≥1 real instance attribute — not an empty shell class.
At least one method beyond __init__ exists and does something with self's state — not a static/unrelated helper.
At least one instance is created and its method called, replacing (or alongside, mid-refactor) the old dict/list-entry representation.

Graded on two separate tracks, not averaged: this Quick Check (fully mechanical) plus a holistic Light Build pass — Strong/Solid/Minimal bands.

What exactly do I turn in? Your world code with the new class defined, instantiated, and used, plus your World Bible entry. The suggested practice problem (one dict/list entity becomes a class with __init__ + one real method) can directly be your gate submission.

What carries forward? Week 13 is Round 2 — at least two of your classes must interact, a method call crossing between two objects, plus your capstone Decide/Compare.
