Reasoning Odyssey Checkpoint 3 — Week 14 — The World Works With Others, and Knows Itself

What are we learning? GitHub/source control, pair programming, and AI-aware coding practice — plus a mandatory Full Trail Debrief that makes the real cost and payoff of Weeks 12–13's class refactor explicit.

What am I building/changing? No new technical-concept deadline — this is a deeper synthesis and consolidation check-in on the prior build. The required new piece is the Full Trail Debrief: what broke when you moved from dict/list entries to classes, why it broke, and what you'd design differently starting from Week 1 with what you know now.

How does my world connect? The Debrief looks directly back at Weeks 10–13's refactor from dict/list entries into interacting classes — the biggest structural change your world has been through.

What evidence do I keep? Log your Debrief answers in the World Bible — this is the mid-semester Judgment Log touchpoint.

Success criteria (qualitative bands — not a point-per-criterion rubric)

Two separately-graded parts, neither pass/fail — this week uses full rubrics, not a Quick Check.

Part 1 — Full Build. The four-axis rubric (Functions / Concept use / Explanation / Demonstrability) — "Concept use" here means real class interaction from Week 13's gate, not just any code running.

BandFull Trail Debrief — what it looks like
StrongNames a specific, real break (or a specific, real reason nothing broke) with a concrete cause tied to an actual Week 10–13 design decision; the "what I'd design differently" answer shows a genuinely different mental model.
SolidReal answers to all three Debrief questions, but the cause analysis or the redesign answer stays generic ("I'd plan better" without saying what that means concretely).
Not yetAnswers are present but don't engage with the actual refactor — could have been written without having done Weeks 12–13 at all.

A student who reports "nothing broke" should not automatically score low — but must explain why it went smoothly (a good Week 10–11 design choice, prior OOP exposure), not just assert it. This is the one week where a thin World Bible log isn't sufficient — the Debrief needs real prose.

What exactly do I turn in? Your consolidated project (full Build), your three-question Full Trail Debrief written out, and this same week's professional-pathway portfolio mid-semester update (already scheduled to pair with this week — no separate assignment).

What carries forward? Week 15 is a light async buffer week — no new technical material; the professional-pathway portfolio is the only required work.
