Reasoning Odyssey Gate — Week 5 — The First Season Passes

What are we learning? Loops and repetition — how to make a program repeat real work an unknown or variable number of times, driven by real data or a stopping condition, instead of typing the same line three times by hand.

What am I building/changing? Add a loop to your world that processes an unknown or variable number of events — sentinel-controlled, or range-driven from real world state (not a hardcoded range(3) with no connection to anything). Something repeated — harvests, patrols, transactions, log entries — happens because the loop says so, not because you wrote it out by hand.

How does my world connect? This is the first week your world can grow on its own — the branching decision you built in Week 3/4 now runs inside a loop that changes your world's state across more than one pass, not just once.

What evidence do I keep? One World Bible line: what gate you passed, what broke.

Quick Check (pass/fail — all three required, no partial credit)

A for or while loop is present and actually repeats — not a single-iteration loop standing in for an if.
The number of iterations is driven by real data or a sentinel condition, not a hardcoded constant unrelated to world state.
An accumulator or running total/state changes across iterations — the loop does more than print the same thing N times.

Graded on two separate tracks, not averaged: this Quick Check (pass/fail, part of the weekly reinforcement category) plus a holistic Light Build pass on your open continuation time — Strong/Solid/Minimal bands, not a point-per-criterion rubric. This is the clearest fully-mechanical gate so far, checkable from your code without a judgment call.

What exactly do I turn in? Your world's code showing the loop in action, plus your one-line World Bible entry. The suggested practice problem (loop over 3–5 made-up events, summing or counting as you go) can directly be your gate submission — you don't need a second, different version.

What carries forward? Week 6 folds this loop directly into Reasoning Odyssey Checkpoint 1 — no separate gate next week; the checkpoint is the gate, and whatever loop-driven behavior you build here is a completely acceptable core to extend lightly rather than start over.
