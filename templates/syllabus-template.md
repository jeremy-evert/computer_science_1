# Reusable syllabus template

The archive supports a recurring syllabus outline: instructor/contact and help channels; course description/prerequisite; ZyBooks and supplemental materials; attendance and participation; late work; communication/support; learning objectives/general education outcomes; chapter/project sequence; grading/activities; accommodations and institutional policies.

Keep the human support language, flexible contact options, and explicit working-code/communication outcomes. Update dates, prices, links, attendance consequences, and policy language each offering.
